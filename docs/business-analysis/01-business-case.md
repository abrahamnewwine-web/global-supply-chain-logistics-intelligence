# Business Case & Problem Statement — Global Supply Chain & Logistics Intelligence

**Document ID:** GSCL-BC-001 | **Author:** Abraham Adetola, PMP®, PMI-PBA®, CSM®, CSPO®

## 1. Business Problem
Global logistics networks face heightened vulnerability from severe weather incidents, port congestion, multi-modal lead-time variability, and route-level geopolitical/operational risks. When shipment volumes, disruption events, carrier performance, and fuel indices sit in separate operational trackers, supply chain leaders cannot proactively triage high-risk corridors.

## 2. Project Objectives
- **BO-01:** Consolidate shipment volumes, disruptions, success rates, lead times, carrier reliability, risk index, and fuel prices into a single executive overview.
- **BO-02:** Quantify weather-driven disruption patterns across Hurricane, Storm, Fog, Rain, and Clear conditions.
- **BO-03:** Benchmark transportation performance across Air, Sea, Road, Rail, product categories, and regions.
- **BO-04:** Identify critical trade corridors (such as `Rotterdam → Marseille` and Antwerp export lanes) for proactive mitigation.
- **BO-05:** Establish governed KPI definitions with interactive filtering and reconciliation controls.
