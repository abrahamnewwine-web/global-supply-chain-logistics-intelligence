# Agile Epics, Features & User Stories — Global Supply Chain & Logistics Intelligence

- **Epic 1 (Shipment Data Governance):** Normalize 5,000 multi-modal shipments across Date, Route, Carrier, Category, and Weather into SQLite (`US-101`).
- **Epic 2 (Disruption & Weather Intelligence):** Surface disruption trends and Hurricane/Storm impact distributions (`US-201`).
- **Epic 3 (Corridor Risk Prioritization):** Rank high-risk lanes, highlight `Rotterdam → Marseille`, and enable 7-slicer filtering + CSV export (`US-301`).
