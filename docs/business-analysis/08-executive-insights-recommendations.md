# Executive Insights & Strategic Recommendations — Global Supply Chain & Logistics Intelligence

**Prepared by:** Abraham Adetola, PMP®, PMI-PBA®, CSM®, CSPO®

1. **Strengthen Shipment Disruption Management (`61.26%` baseline exposure):** Implement exception thresholds and automated escalation workflows for high-disruption lanes.
2. **Risk-Based Route Prioritization (`31.42%` high-risk shipments):** Prioritize contingency capacity and carrier diversification on `Rotterdam → Marseille` (`~27.76d` avg lead time) and high-volume Antwerp export routes.
3. **Weather Disruption Preparedness:** Hurricane (`990` disrupted) and Storm (`797` disrupted) represent the top weather drivers; integrate meteorological alerts into dispatch scheduling.
4. **Lead-Time & KPI Governance:** Standardize disruption, reliability, and high-risk definitions across all regional operations before deploying live TMS integrations.
